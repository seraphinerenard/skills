The close becomes one list that runs itself

Every task knows its owner, its evidence, and what it blocks downstream. Controllers watch one screen where every task reports its own state.

Ledgerline connects to your general ledger and your bank feeds on day one; the first close maps the checklist you already run, line for line.

01

Connect the ledger

Read-only connections to NetSuite, Sage Intacct, and QuickBooks pull balances every hour. Nothing writes back without an approval.

02

Map the checklist

Your current close tasks import from a spreadsheet in one pass; each line gets an owner, a due day, and the account it certifies.

03

Reconcile with evidence

Bank matches, subledger ties, and flux explanations attach to the task that requires them, where the auditor will look for them later.

04

Certify and archive

The controller signs each account once its tasks are complete, and the whole month exports as a single audit binder.
