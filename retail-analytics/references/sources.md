## Inventory record inaccuracy and on-shelf availability

- DeHoratius, N. and Raman, A., "Inventory Record Inaccuracy: An Empirical Analysis", Management Science 54(4), 2008, pp. 627-641. Audited ~370,000 records across 37 stores of one retailer; 65% of records were inaccurate; 26.4% of the variance in inaccuracy sits between product categories and 2.7% between stores. https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.1070.0789 (accessed 2026-07-12).
- Gruen, T.W., Corsten, D. and Bharadwaj, S., "Retail Out-of-Stocks: A Worldwide Examination of Causes, Rates, and Consumer Responses", Grocery Manufacturers of America, 2002. Worldwide average out-of-stock rate 8.3% in FMCG across developed economies; about 72% of out-of-stocks trace to in-store ordering and replenishment practice; lost sales from OOS around 3.9% globally and 3.8% in the US. Reference record: https://www.scirp.org/reference/referencespapers?referenceid=1363882 (accessed 2026-07-12); companion practitioner guide: https://www.nacds.org/pdfs/membership/out_of_stock.pdf (accessed 2026-07-12); summary of consumer responses: Corsten and Gruen, "Stock-Outs Cause Walkouts", Harvard Business Review, May 2004, https://hbr.org/2004/05/stock-outs-cause-walkouts (accessed 2026-07-12). The commonly quoted consumer-response split from this research is: 31% buy the item at another store, 26% substitute a different brand, 19% substitute within the brand, 15% delay the purchase, 9% do not buy. VERIFY the exact split against the 2002 report before client use; the in-session search confirmed 45% total substitution and the 8.3% and 72% figures.
- Agrawal, N. and Smith, S.A., "Estimating negative binomial demand for retail inventory management with unobservable lost sales", Naval Research Logistics 43(6), 1996, pp. 839-861. On data from a major retail chain, negative binomial fits SKU-level demand better than Poisson or normal. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/(SICI)1520-6750(199609)43:6%3C839::AID-NAV4%3E3.0.CO;2-5 (accessed 2026-07-12 via the demand-forecasting research sheet cited below).
- Chen, L., "Fixing Phantom Stockouts: Optimal Data-Driven Shelf Inspection Policies", 2021. Data-driven inspection finds lost sales of about 3.0% versus 0.2% when the inventory record is trusted. https://www.researchgate.net/publication/345980410_Fixing_Phantom_Stockouts_Optimal_Data-Driven_Shelf_Inspection_Policies (accessed 2026-07-12; ResearchGate-only sourcing, thin).
- FreshRetailNet-50K (Dingdong Ltd. and Sun Yat-Sen University), arXiv 2505.16319, 2025. 50,000 store-SKU hourly series with stockout labels; raw sales underestimate demand by 7.37% (weighted percentage error); hourly stockout rates run from under 2% at 06:00 to 26% by 20:00. https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16319 (accessed 2026-07-12).
- NRF National Retail Security Survey: shrink around 1.6% of retail sales (roughly US$112 billion) for 2022. VERIFY: figure from prior knowledge, no in-session fetch; the survey is republished annually at https://nrf.com.
- The censored-demand and unconstraining literature (Nahmias 1994; Sachs and Minner 2014; Jain, Rudi and Wang 2015; Vulcano, van Ryzin and Ratliff 2012; Tobit Kalman filtering, Trapero et al. 2024; the Amazon glance-view correction, Madeka et al. 2022; the Zalando masking practice, Kunz et al. 2023) is catalogued with URLs and access dates in the demand-forecasting skill: /path/to/skills/demand-forecasting/references/research/censored-demand-unconstraining.md (compiled 2026-07-12). Cite that sheet for anything on lost-sales estimation methods.

## Insurgent and challenger brands

- Bain & Company, "Insurgent Brands Steal the Spotlight in 2025", https://www.bain.com/insights/insurgent-brands-2025-snap-chart/ (accessed 2026-07-12). Selection criteria: more than US$25 million annual revenue in tracked channels, growth more than 10x the category average over the past five years, positive growth over the past two years, and independence (or acquisition by a large CPG within the past two years). Insurgents held under 2% of total market share while capturing about 36% of tracked-channel market growth in 2025 and about 39% of incremental category growth in 2024 (17% in 2023).
- Bain & Company, "2026 US Insurgent Brands: Powering the Next Wave of Growth", https://www.bain.com/insights/2026-us-insurgent-brands-powering-the-next-wave-of-growth-snap-chart/ (accessed 2026-07-12). The 2026 edition raises the revenue floor to more than US$35 million in NielsenIQ-tracked channels.
- Bain & Company press release, 10th annual Insurgent Brands list, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/brands-to-watch-bain--company-unveils-its-10th-annual-insurgent-brands-list-302703337.html (accessed 2026-07-12).
- Circana formation: IRI and NPD Group merged in August 2022 and rebranded as Circana in March 2023. VERIFY: dates from prior knowledge; https://www.circana.com.
- Syndicated coverage gaps (Costco, Trader Joe's, and Aldi do not provide item-level POS to NielsenIQ or Circana; Amazon is covered only partially through separate e-commerce measurement): practitioner consensus as of the mid-2020s, from prior knowledge. VERIFY per engagement; coverage contracts change.
