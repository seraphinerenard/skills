(https://retailtechinnovationhub.com/home/2024/5/28/demand-based-optimisation-at-scale-using-ai, accessed 2026-07-12)

# CROSS-VENDOR SYNTHESIS

Reinforcement learning: none of the three vendors claims RL in production pricing in its own primary materials. The single RL attribution to any of them is a partner page (Pricer) describing Revionics, which is UNVERIFIED against Revionics' own statements. All three center on price-elasticity models plus demand forecasting plus an optimizer, and all three now layer on generative/agentic AI (Revionics multi-agent on Vertex AI, o9 GenAI Digital Brain, Blue Yonder AI/ML lifecycle pricing).
Independent verification: no [INDEPENDENT] third-party-audited margin or revenue lift figures exist for any of the three. Every lift percentage above is [VENDOR-MARKETING], including the numbers Revionics attributes to BCG, Coresight, and Bain (those appear only inside Revionics' own whitepaper, not fetched from the analysts directly).
Cleanest named-client numbers: Revionics/Farmacorp (10% YoY revenue growth, 108% of profit plan) is the most concrete named-client disclosure across all three vendors. Blue Yonder and o9 named-client pages (Morrisons, bonprix, o9's anonymized retailers) are largely qualitative for the pricing/markdown product specifically.
Data-quality flags: the Revionics ROI whitepaper is undated (its agentic-AI content places it in 2025); Blue Yonder's now.blueyonder.com pricing pages are being retired (several 404/redirect), so its quoted numbers may move; the Gurobi Blue Yonder case-study page 404'd; and o9solutions.com blocked all direct fetches (403), so o9 quotes rely on search extraction.
