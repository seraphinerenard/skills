## The two ERP schools

<!-- allow:D2 the figures on these lines carry their dates and sources -->
- Damodaran implied ERP: 4.23% over the 10-year (January 1, 2026 anchor;
  4.46% as the US premium over his default-free rate), updated mid-2026 to
  4.17% mature-market and 4.45% US (July 1, 2026).
- Kroll recommended US ERP: 5.0% (cut from 5.5% effective September 2,
  2025), over the higher of a 3.5% normalized risk-free rate or the spot
  20-year Treasury; at the July 2026 20-year of ~5.07% the spot governs, so
  Kroll's beta-1 cost of equity reads near 10.1% against Damodaran's ~8.7%.
- KPMG's DACH cost-of-capital study (to mid-2025): average applied WACC
  8.5%, market risk premium 6.7%, risk-free 2.5%, euro-based; the ~2-point
  gap against Damodaran-school numbers is methodology and currency, and a
  comps memo says which school it stands in.

## Multiples

- Trading EV/EBITDA (Damodaran, January 2026, positive-EBITDA firms):
  software 24-30x, semiconductors 34.8x, machinery 16.2x, food processing
  10.0x, utilities 13.7x, retail 17.4x, E&P 5.2x, total market 19.7x.
- Transactions: strategic-M&A median 11.6x EV/EBITDA for 2025 (Bain 2026
  report, flat year over year); mid-2026 trailing global median ~10.7x;
  PE-led deals ~12.6x against ~9.8x for corporate buyers; US PE buyout
  median ~12x with $1B+ deals at 15.5x (2024) and the middle market ~9.6x.
- Software revenue multiples depend entirely on the universe: broad public
  software median ~2.3x EV/NTM revenue (July 2026); SaaS Capital Index
  ~3.2-4.8x ARR (June 2026, range across trackers, flagged); Bessemer cloud
  index median ~6.1x (June 2026); the 2021 peak on the SaaS Capital Index
  was 16.9x, so current levels sit roughly 70-80% below it.

## Premia, discounts, and conventions

- Control premium: US median ~28% in 2024 (10-year median 27%); technology
  deals ran a ~17% median in 2024 (FactSet/BVR).
- DLOM for private companies: 15-40% range, most commonly 20-35%;
  restricted-stock studies centre near a 24% mean.
- Kroll CRSP decile size premia remain in use for build-ups; the current
  decile values sit behind the Cost of Capital Navigator paywall
  (unverified here), and subdividing decile 10 into micro-cap slices draws
  published criticism because the smallest listed firms are small for a
  reason.
- Terminal growth: anchor below long-run nominal GDP; CBO's February 2026
  outlook implies a US nominal ceiling near 3.8-4.0% (1.8% real, ~2% PCE),
  and practice sets terminal g at 2-3%.
- Mid-year convention appeared in roughly 75% of 2024 SEC fairness opinions
  and lifts a DCF 3-5% against year-end discounting; use it, and say so.

## Private-market debt

Large-corporate LBO debt ran ~4.7x pro-forma debt/EBITDA in Q1 2025 with
equity contributions in the 45-55% band; all-in floating borrowing
approximates SOFR (3.53%) plus single-B term-loan spreads of ~3.5-4.5%, so
~7-8%, with the spread add-on flagged in the sheet as a market-range
estimate on top of the verified SOFR print.
